Applications

Where ground cover matters

Applications include quantifying residue cover after tillage and planting, soil erosion and water conservation studies, soil evaporation studies, soil carbon studies, and ground truth for satellite and drone estimates of ground cover.

How it works

Shoot, load, classify, export.

  1. 01

    Take photos

    Use any phone or camera. Hold it at waist height and point it straight down at about one square meter of ground.

  2. 02

    Load images

    Load one or many JPG or PNG files. Stover reads the capture time, GPS coordinates, and device from each image’s EXIF data.

  3. 03

    Classify

    A neural network classifies every pixel as residue, canopy, or soil at 1024 × 1024 pixels (about 1 mm per pixel for photos taken about 1 m above the ground).

  4. 04

    Export

    Export data as CSV, classified images, or a PDF report.

Field guide

Taking good images

Do

  • Take images at waist height (about 1 m or 3 ft)
  • Point the camera straight down
  • Take images in good illumination
  • Keep the frame clear of other objects
  • Make sure the image is sharp, not blurry

Don’t

  • Take images from shoulder height or higher
  • Tilt the camera
  • Include specular reflections (glare)
  • Include shoes, stakes, or flags in the frame
  • Zoom in
